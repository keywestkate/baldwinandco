The 30-Day Launch Blueprint

Scattered to Streamlined in Under a Month

Here is the exact, scannable breakdown of how we take your brand from a scattered digital footprint to a polished, live, AI-optimized online presence in less than 30 days.

Phase 1 | Days 1–2 The Asset Dump & Onboarding The Goal

Gathering your raw material without the friction of long meetings or endless typing.

The Process

You complete our frictionless AI Voice-Text Questionnaire. We harvest your existing flyers, marketing assets, and scattered notes to build your foundational brand profile.

Phase 2 | Days 3–7 The Photography Lock The Goal

Securing the high-end visuals that anchor your entire aesthetic.

The Process

Your photographer delivers 10–14 high-quality anchor images. If you don't have a photographer, our emergency creator backup is immediately deployed to keep us on schedule.

Phase 3 | Days 8–15 Core Architectural Build The Goal

Turning a messy marketing history into a premium digital experience.

The Process

We design and build our signature 3-page website framework — Home, Services, About — using modern, editorial typography and high-conversion layouts.

Phase 4 | Days 16–20 SEO & GEO Infrastructure The Goal

Heavy-duty technical cleanup and future-proofing.

The Process

We map your custom domains, optimize image weights for lightning-fast mobile speed, and structure your site data so AI search engines — like ChatGPT and Google Gemini — can easily scrape, surface, and recommend your business.

Phase 5 | Day 21 The Turnkey Launch The Goal

Going live.

The Process

Your brand-new, polished website officially launches to the public.

Phase 6 | Days 22–52 30-Day Post-Launch Support & Hand-Off The Goal

Total backend independence and peace of mind.

The Process

A 30-day post-launch grace period where we handle any standard text or image updates, paired with a custom video training library so you fully own and control your new digital asset.

POLISHED. STRATEGIC. YOURS.
