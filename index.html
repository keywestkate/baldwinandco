The Process

01. The AI Voice Onboarding
02. The Gaps & Strategy Session
03. The Rebuild & Elevate
04. The Turnkey Launch & Hand-Off

Step 01: The AI Voice Onboarding

Day One

No long forms. No typing. We send you a simple questionnaire and you answer it using your phone's voice-to-text with ChatGPT — just talk through your business like you're explaining it to a friend.

This gives us everything we need to get started. Your story, your goals, your existing materials. All captured in your own words, in minutes.

Step 02: The Gaps & Strategy Session

Week 1

We review your old flyers, bios, social profiles, and any existing marketing materials. We map exactly what is working, what is outdated, and what is invisible to search engines and AI tools.

You leave this session with a clear picture of what gets kept, what gets refreshed, and what gets built from the ground up.

Step 03: The Rebuild & Elevate

Weeks 2–4

This is where the transformation happens. Over 3 to 4 weeks, we redesign your visual identity, rewrite your messaging, build your web presence, and optimize every digital touchpoint for AI and geo-based search visibility.

Your business keeps running normally the entire time. We work around your schedule — no disruption, no downtime.

Step 04: The Turnkey Launch & Hand-Off

Week 4–5

We go live together. You receive a fully completed brand system, a working website, video walkthrough guides, and every login and asset organized and handed directly to you.

No monthly retainer. No agency dependency. You own everything and your team can manage it all independently from day one.
