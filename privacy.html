Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 1, 2026

Baldwin & Co. ("we," "our," or "us") is a boutique brand strategy and digital design studio operated by Kate Baldwin. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information submitted through our website at baldwinandco.com.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you fill out our contact form. This includes your name, email address, phone number, business name, and any details you share in your message. We do not collect payment information directly through this website.

How We Use Your Information

The information you submit is used solely to respond to your inquiry, discuss your project needs, and communicate with you about services offered by Baldwin & Co. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may use your email address to follow up on your inquiry or send information directly relevant to your project. We do not add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent.

Contact Form Processing

Our contact form is processed through Formspree, a third-party form service. Information submitted through the form is transmitted to Formspree's servers and then forwarded to our email. Formspree's privacy practices govern their handling of that data. You can review their policy at formspree.io.

Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics services. We may use basic browser session behavior to improve site performance, but we do not build profiles or track visitors across sessions or sites.

Data Retention

Inquiry information is retained in our email inbox only as long as necessary to manage an ongoing or potential client relationship. We do not maintain a formal database of prospect records.

Your Rights

You may request that we delete any personal information you have provided to us by contacting us at kate@keywestkate.com. We will honor deletion requests promptly.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out directly: kate@keywestkate.com
