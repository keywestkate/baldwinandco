The Property

A Rare Waterfront Find in the Lower Keys

Welcome to waterfront living in the Florida Keys. This newly constructed, unfurnished 4-bedroom, 3-bath concrete home offers approximately 1,896 square feet of bright, modern living space with beautiful open-water views.

The home features tile flooring throughout, impact-resistant windows and doors, quartz countertops, stainless-steel appliances, a metal roof, and private porches designed to enjoy the surrounding coastal scenery.

Boaters will appreciate the concrete dock, boat lift, and quick access to deep water, making it easy to reach the ocean, backcountry, and the waters surrounding Bahia Honda Bridge.
